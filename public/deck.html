01
Writing copy by hand is slow and inconsistent
Push, WhatsApp, Email, and In-App each have different rules — and it's easy to mix them up
Writers spend 30–60 minutes per brief just on first drafts, before any reviews happen
Tone varies writer to writer — the brand voice drifts without a shared reference
There's no easy way to see what copy has been written, spot patterns, or track what's working
02
What it is
A web-based tool, accessible to approved ACKO writers via Google sign-in
You pick a channel, product, user state, and drop in a brief — and get 3 ready-to-use copy variants
Every output follows ACKO's tone of voice and word rules — automatically, every time

03
It doesn't just send a prompt to AI.
It layers five types of intelligence.
Layer 1
Tone of Voice Rules
ACKO's brand dos and don'ts, injected into every single generation
Layer 2
Word Rules
Banned words auto-replaced with preferred ACKO alternatives
Layer 3
Feedback Memory
Real copy rated by writers — used as examples for future outputs
Layer 4 + 5
Learned Patterns
Patterns from high-rated copy + what writers change after the first draft

The more writers use and rate the tool, the better it gets — for everyone. Signals from one writer improve output for the whole team.

04
Two layers of quality control
Prompt Layer
Tone of voice rules and word rules are written into every prompt before generation starts
The AI is instructed to follow them — and in most cases, it does
Sanitisation Layer
After generation, every output is scanned and banned terms are replaced automatically
This runs 100% of the time — regardless of what the AI does

Certain words will never appear in generated copy. The second layer is deterministic — it's not a guideline, it's a hard filter.
