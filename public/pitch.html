The problem

Copy briefs take too long — and too much gets left on the table.

⏱
20–40 min per brief
A single push notification brief — drafting, iterating, checking brand fit — eats up nearly an hour of a writer's time.
🔁
One angle explored
Writers rarely have time to explore multiple psychological hooks or frameworks per brief. The first decent option usually ships.
📋
No consistent quality bar
No structured way to evaluate copy quality before it goes out. Judgement is entirely subjective and person-dependent.
🧩
Context not captured
Every brief starts from scratch. Past copy, what worked, what didn't — none of it is systematically fed back in.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

Three things make this different.

  • 1
    It knows ACKO's context. Generic GPT doesn't know our tone of voice, what NCB means to a renewal customer, or that push copy has a 33/75 character limit. All of that is baked in here.
  • 2
    It gives structure, not just text. GPT gives you prose. This gives you scored, structured variants — headline vs. body split, CRM quality scores across 8 dimensions, a live phone preview.
  • 3
    The output is decision-ready. No prompt engineering, no reformatting, no brand-checking. Brief in, variants out — feedback, refinement, and export all in one place.
